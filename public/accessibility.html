Accessibility

Last reviewed November 9, 2026

Everyone should be able to check the line and read the rules, on any device and with any assistive technology. This site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, the standard used by the University of Florida and by the U.S. Department of Justice's rule for public institutions' websites.

What we do

  • Every page works with a keyboard alone, with visible focus and a "Skip to content" link.
  • Screen readers announce changes to the line count and urgent notices as they happen.
  • Text and controls meet WCAG color-contrast requirements, and color is never the only way information is shown.
  • Pages zoom and reflow to small screens without losing content.
  • Images have text alternatives, and decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
  • If your device asks for reduced motion, the counter's animations are turned off.
  • The page doesn't time out, flash, or play sound.

How it's checked

Every page is tested with the open-source axe accessibility engine against WCAG 2.1 A and AA rules, on phone and desktop screen sizes, and checked with keyboard-only use.

If something on this site is hard to use, please let us know and we'll fix it. Email the club at . Students can also get help from the UF Disability Resource Center, and the university's accessibility resources are at accessibility.ufl.edu.
